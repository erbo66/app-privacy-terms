# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

This policy describes how the video editing and clipping app (the “App”) handles information. The App is currently provided for testing and app verification; features and this policy may change as development continues.

## Video and other information

Video editing and clipping are designed to be processed locally on your device. Your source videos and exported clips are not uploaded by the App unless a feature clearly asks you to send or share them. Your device or platform may independently handle data when you use sharing, cloud backup, or other third-party services; their privacy terms apply.

The App does not sell personal data. We do not use your videos to build advertising profiles. If a future feature collects or transmits information, we will explain that in the App and update this policy.

## Storage and security

Projects, source media, and exports may remain in storage you choose on your device. You control their deletion through the App or your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Children and changes

The App is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy as the App develops. The current version will be published at this URL.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the App publisher through the contact information on the App’s store listing.
